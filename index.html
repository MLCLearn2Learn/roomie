Our privacy promise

Built so principals can say yes.

Roomie keeps as little as possible about your students, and never records your classroom.

  • No accounts, no logins. Your class list and plan stay in the browser on your computer.
  • First names only. Roomie never asks for surnames, birthdays or photos.
  • Nothing is recorded. The noise meter and voice commands listen live. No audio is ever saved or sent.
  • Student answers are anonymous by default and deleted from Roomie’s Sydney server after 24 hours.
  • Moving computers? Your Roomie is locked with your code before it leaves, so even Roomie can’t read it.
