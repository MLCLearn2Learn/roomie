Our privacy promise

Clear choices about classroom data.

Your everyday board works with data saved in your browser. Optional connected features have different data needs, explained in Setup → Privacy, backup & move.

  • No accounts, no logins. Your class list and plan stay in the browser on your computer.
  • First names only. Roomie never asks for surnames, birthdays or photos.
  • Microphone choices. Roomie does not save an audio recording. Voice commands use on-device recognition by default; optional cloud recognition sends audio to the browser’s speech service. Coaching can save recognised text.
  • Student answers are anonymous by default and sent to Roomie’s server when pupils submit. Feelings have no name attached. Server retention should be checked before classroom use.
  • Moving computers? Your saved data is encrypted before upload. Anyone with the move code can open it, so keep that code private.
Questions teachers ask

Before you try it

Is it really free?

Yes. Roomie is free for founding classrooms while we build it with teachers. No card, no trial clock.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is needed. Open Roomie in a browser on the computer connected to your board or TV. Microphone, video and connected features depend on your browser and school settings.

How long does setup take?

About three minutes. Paste your class list straight from a spreadsheet or your school system, type your recess and lunch times, and Roomie builds your week.

My days are all different. Will it cope?

Yes. Set each weekday separately, and change “just today” or “just tomorrow” for assemblies, excursions and carnivals without touching your usual plan.

What about student data?

Your class display names, plan and activity summaries are saved in your browser. No student accounts are needed. Optional student-device answers go to Roomie’s server, anonymously unless names are requested; feelings do not include a name. Coaching can save recognised speech, and optional cloud speech recognition sends audio to your browser’s speech service. See Setup → Privacy, backup & move for the details and check server retention before using connected features with a class.

I only want the tools, not the timetable.

Choose “Just the toolkit” and Roomie becomes a big-button board of timers, brain breaks, tickets and more, that only does what you tap.

Can a relief teacher use it?

Yes. Roomie keeps running the day, and the relief teacher page prints your plan, routines and notes.
